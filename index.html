About Deskora

Designed for Calm, Focused Productivity

Deskora is an Indian brand committed to helping students, young professionals, and remote workers build functional, beautiful workspaces. We believe an orderly desk encourages clear thinking and better daily focus.

From ergonomic risers that reduce physical strain to durable vegan leather mats that protect your desk, every product is selected to deliver lasting value and everyday practicality.

Founded by Tanishk Ghadiya — 2nd year PGDM student at FOSTIIMA Business School, Delhi — Deskora was created from firsthand student experience to eliminate desk clutter, neck strain, and disorganisation with accessible, quality workspace essentials.

Practical Ergonomics

Products that lift screens, support wrists, and arrange essentials for comfortable posture-friendly work sessions.

Durable Materials

Sturdy aluminium alloys, stain-resistant vegan leather, and reinforced polymers built for everyday Indian use.
