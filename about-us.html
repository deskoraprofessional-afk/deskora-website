Our Story & Purpose

Designed for Calm, Focused Productivity

Deskora is an Indian e-commerce brand committed to helping students, young professionals, and remote workers create functional and aesthetic workspaces. We believe an orderly desk encourages clear thinking and better daily focus.

Why We Started Deskora

The idea behind Deskora began with a simple observation: millions of Indian students, MBA aspirants, and remote workers spend upwards of 8 to 12 hours a day seated at their desks, yet most workspaces are cluttered, uncomfortable, and uninspiring.

Laptops resting flat on tables force users into poor, hunched postures that cause chronic neck and shoulder fatigue. Tangled charging wires, scattered stationery, and scratched table surfaces create ongoing subconscious friction.

We created Deskora to offer thoughtfully curated, premium-grade desk accessories tailored specifically for modern Indian setups. From durable aircraft-grade aluminum laptop stands to dual-sided vegan leather desk blotters, every product is selected for daily durability, ergonomic comfort, and clean visual design.

Guiding Principles

What Defines Every Deskora Product

Our design philosophy prioritises everyday comfort, honest material construction, and straightforward customer service.

1. Practical Ergonomics

We design and curate risers, docks, and wrist rests that encourage neutral posture, alleviate spinal pressure, and keep devices properly ventilated during marathon work sessions.

2. Honest, Durable Materials

We insist on genuine aviation-grade aluminum alloys, sustainable Moso bamboo, and heavy-duty bio-based vegan leather. No cheap, flimsy plastics that sag or break after a month.

3. Calming Visual Aesthetics

Visual clutter leads to mental fatigue. Our neutral palette of deep greens, warm terracotta accents, and soft beige tones elevates your workspace into a calm, focused sanctuary.

4. Pan-India Fulfillment

With operational fulfillment hubs in Bengaluru and Gurugram, we dispatch orders swiftly across India, ensuring dependable packaging and responsive customer assistance.

Founded by a Student, Built for Students

The Mind Behind Deskora

Meet the Founder

Tanishk Ghadiya

Founder & Curator, Deskora

2nd Year PGDM Student — FOSTIIMA Business School, New Delhi

Deskora was born out of a very real, very personal frustration. As a PGDM student navigating long study sessions, back-to-back lectures, and endless case studies at FOSTIIMA Business School, I found myself constantly battling a cluttered, uncomfortable desk.

My laptop sat flat on the table, forcing me to hunch forward for hours. Cables snaked across my study surface. Stationery was always missing when I needed it. I looked for solutions online — but most products were either overpriced imports, cheap plastic junk, or completely irrelevant to the Indian student context.

That gap is exactly what Deskora fills. I curated a focused range of workspace essentials — ergonomic stands, quality desk mats, wooden organisers — that are genuinely built for people like us: students, young professionals, and remote workers in India who deserve a smarter, calmer desk without spending a fortune.

“A well-organised desk is not a luxury — it is the foundation of focused thinking. Deskora exists to make that accessible for every student and professional in India.” — Tanishk Ghadiya

Why Deskora Exists

The Real Problems We Solve

Every product in the Deskora range was chosen to fix a specific pain point that students and professionals face every single day at their desks.

Neck & Posture Fatigue from Flat Laptops

Staring down at a flat laptop for 6–10 hours a day causes chronic neck strain and poor spinal posture. Deskora's aluminium laptop risers and foldable stands elevate screens to eye level, reducing fatigue instantly.

Cluttered, Disorganised Desks

Scattered pens, charging cables, sticky notes, and stationery create constant visual and mental clutter. Our wooden desk organisers and modular trays give every item a dedicated, tidy home — instantly.

Scratched & Unprotected Desk Surfaces

Sliding laptops, rough-bottomed mugs, and sharp stationery scratch and stain table surfaces over time. Deskora's premium desk mats and vegan leather blotters protect your desk while creating a smooth, friction-free work zone.

Cable Chaos & Tangled Wires

Tangled charging cables, earphone wires, and USB leads are a constant frustration. Deskora's magnetic silicone cable clips and cable organiser sleeves keep every wire routed, labelled, and tangle-free — in seconds.

No Stable Phone or Tablet Stand

Propping your phone against a book to attend a Zoom call or watch a lecture is frustrating and unstable. Deskora's wooden mobile stands and adjustable phone holders provide a stable, angle-perfect cradle for every device.

Overpriced or Low-Quality Indian Market Options

Most desk accessories available in India are either cheap, flimsy products that break within weeks, or expensive imported brands. Deskora bridges this gap — premium materials and design at honest, student-friendly Indian prices.
