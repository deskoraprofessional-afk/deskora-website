The Science of Ergonomic Screen Positioning

Neutral Neck Posture

Looking down at a flat laptop forces your neck to bear up to 27 kg of gravitational head weight. Raising the screen to eye level preserves natural spinal curvature.

Thermal Heat Dissipation

Laptops resting directly on table surfaces trap heat, causing processor thermal throttling. Open aluminum stands promote natural airflow to keep laptops cool.

Keyboard Clearance

Elevating the laptop frees up the desk surface beneath it, allowing you to position an external mechanical keyboard and mouse comfortably within arm's reach.
