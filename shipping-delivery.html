Shipping & Delivery Details

Clear, predictable delivery timelines for study tables and home offices across India.

1. Order Processing & Dispatch

Every Deskora order is carefully inspected, packed with protective materials, and dispatched within 1 to 2 business days (Monday through Saturday, excluding public holidays).

Shipments originate from our primary fulfillment operations located in Bengaluru and Gurugram, ensuring swift transit across North, South, West, and East India.

2. Estimated Delivery Timelines

Delivery transit times depend on your destination address in India:

  • Metro Cities (Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai): 3 to 5 business days after dispatch.
  • Tier 2 & Tier 3 Cities / State Capitals: 4 to 7 business days after dispatch.
  • Regional Towns & North-East / J&K: 6 to 9 business days after dispatch.

3. Logistics Partners & Tracking

We work with India's leading surface and air courier networks, including Blue Dart, Delhivery, DTDC, and Xpressbees.

As soon as your package is scanned into the courier facility, you will receive an automatic tracking link via WhatsApp and email so you can track your package every step of the journey.

4. Transparent Shipping Charges

We keep our delivery pricing simple and transparent:

  • Free Pan-India Delivery: On all orders of ₹999 and above, including all curated workspace setup kits.
  • Standard Delivery (Orders under ₹999): Flat ₹99 across India.
  • No Hidden Surcharges: The price confirmed with you is all-inclusive of standard courier handling and taxes.
